# What is The Drift?

The Drift is a procedural visual sandbox and virtual environment centered on exploration, interaction, and player-driven experience.

It has a physical reality and an inhabited setting, but the experience is not meant to be reduced to a fixed catalog of species, classes, districts, customs, or encounter tables.

The world can become specific through play. What has not been established remains open.

This document is intentionally conservative. Stable setting material belongs here; particular encounters and discoveries belong in Experiences.
