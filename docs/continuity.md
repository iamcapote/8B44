# Continuity

The Drift can preserve continuity without forcing every play session into one universal history.

- **Core setting** — durable facts intentionally shared across the project.
- **Personal continuity** — the history of a particular ongoing experience.
- **Community records** — material people chose to publish in this archive.
- **Unexplored possibility** — what has not been fixed by the current experience.

A merged contribution is a durable public record. It does not automatically force another player's Drift to incorporate it.

When an entry depends on a particular continuity, use the optional `continuity` field.
