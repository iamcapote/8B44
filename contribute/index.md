# Contribute

The archive grows through Pull Requests. You can do the entire process in GitHub's web interface.

## Five steps

1. **Fork** `iamcapote/8B44`.
2. Open `templates/` and copy the closest template.
3. Add it to `_experiences/`, `_characters/`, or `_tips/`.
4. Fill it out and commit it in your fork.
5. Click **Contribute → Open pull request**.

For exact conventions, read [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/iamcapote/8B44/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

A merge accepts the contribution into the archive. It does not make a personal continuity universal.
